Privacy Policy

How we handle information you share with Jovick Travel & Tours.

Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. Information we collect

When you contact us or request a tour, we may collect details you submit, such as your name, email address, phone number, preferred travel date, number of travellers, flight preference, destination interests and message. Please do not submit payment-card details through our forms.

2. How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, prepare or clarify travel proposals, manage booking requests, communicate about a trip and improve our customer service. We do not claim a booking is confirmed merely because a form has been submitted.

3. Service providers

Website form submissions may be processed by the service providers that operate our website and messaging infrastructure. Where necessary to plan a trip, relevant details may be shared with travel suppliers only for the purpose of responding to your request or arranging services, subject to applicable requirements.

4. Payments

Bank-transfer details and payment notifications are used to help our team reconcile a reported payment. A notification is not proof that funds have cleared. Card checkout is not currently available on this website; never send card numbers, security codes, passwords or one-time passcodes through a contact form or chat.

5. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to handle your enquiry, provide requested services, maintain business records and meet applicable obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you submitted, request a correction, or raise a privacy concern. We will respond in accordance with applicable law and any identity checks reasonably needed to protect your information.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, email jovickuniqueenterprises@gmail.com or call +234 915 895 0008.

This page describes the website's general practices and is not legal advice. It should be reviewed and updated if the business's data practices or applicable legal requirements change.
