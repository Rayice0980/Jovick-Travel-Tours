Submitting a contact form or tour booking form sends a request to Jovick Travel & Tours. Our team must confirm the itinerary, availability, final price and applicable terms.

Prices shown on the website are indicative estimates unless a written proposal clearly states otherwise. Prices and availability can change based on travel dates, passenger numbers, exchange rates, supplier rates and the selected arrangements. Flights, accommodation, meals, transfers, activities, insurance and visa fees are included only when expressly listed in the confirmed proposal.

3. Customer responsibilities

Customers are responsible for providing accurate traveller details and ensuring that passports, visas, health documents and other travel requirements are valid and obtained in time. Entry decisions are made by the relevant authorities; no visa or border outcome is guaranteed.

4. Payment and confirmation

Follow the payment instructions issued by our team and verify the account name, account number and final amount before transferring. A website payment notification only tells our team that you report having paid; it does not verify funds or confirm the booking. A booking is considered confirmed only after our team provides confirmation in writing and any agreed payment conditions are met.

5. Flight arrangements

If a package is quoted with a one-way flight, the return journey is the customer's responsibility unless the written proposal says otherwise. Confirm whether the selected proposal includes return flights before paying.

6. Supplier changes and disruptions

Airlines, hotels and other providers may change schedules, availability or services. If a disruption affects a proposed or confirmed trip, contact our team promptly so we can explain the options available under the relevant supplier rules and applicable law.

7. Cancellation, changes and refunds

Cancellation, date-change and refund rights depend on the confirmed proposal, supplier conditions and applicable law. Ask for the applicable written cancellation and refund terms before making payment. See our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

8. Contact

For clarification before booking, email jovickuniqueenterprises@gmail.com or use our Contact page.
