Cancellation & Refund Policy

How to contact us if you need to change or cancel a trip.

Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. Before you pay

Request and read the cancellation, amendment and refund terms that apply to your specific itinerary before making payment. Conditions may differ by airline, hotel, tour operator, ticket type, travel date and other supplier arrangements.

2. Requesting a change or cancellation

Contact Jovick Travel & Tours as soon as possible using the details on our Contact page. Include your name, destination, intended travel date and booking reference if one has been issued. Do not send full payment-card details or account passwords.

3. Refund eligibility and timing

Any refund depends on the written terms confirmed for your booking, the amounts recoverable from suppliers, and applicable law. Supplier penalties, non-refundable tickets or services, and administrative steps may affect eligibility and timing where permitted. We will explain the applicable conditions and any confirmed refund process for your case; this page does not promise a particular refund amount or timeframe.

4. Changes by suppliers or circumstances outside our control

If a supplier changes or cancels a service, or an event outside the parties' reasonable control affects travel, contact our team. Available alternatives will depend on the supplier's rules, the booking terms and applicable law.

5. Payment verification

Clicking “I have made a payment” only sends a payment notification. We must verify receipt of funds separately. Do not assume a booking or refund is complete until our team confirms it in writing.

6. Questions

Email jovickuniqueenterprises@gmail.com or call +234 915 895 0008 for guidance on the terms applicable to your booking.

This is general website information, not legal advice. The business should confirm its actual cancellation rules and have this policy reviewed for applicable requirements before relying on it as a binding policy.
