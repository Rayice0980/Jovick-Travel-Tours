Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you understand our tour options, booking process, and payment steps.

NEED TO KNOW?

We've answered a few common questions.

Open a question below to read the answer. If you need information specific to your trip, please contact our team before making payment.

How do I book a tour?

Browse the Tours page, select the tour you are interested in, and complete the booking details requested. Review your information and the package details carefully before proceeding.

Are the prices shown on the website final?

Prices shown are estimates and may need confirmation before a booking is finalised. Contact us to confirm the current package price, what is included, travel dates, and any additional costs that may apply.

What payment methods can I choose?

The booking flow may display bank transfer and card payment options. Bank transfer instructions are shown on the payment page. Card checkout is not active yet, so do not enter card details on this website. Please contact us if you need help choosing a payment method.

What should I do after making a bank transfer?

Return to the payment page and click “I have made a payment” to notify us. This button reports that you say you have paid; it does not verify the transfer or automatically confirm your booking. Your payment must be checked separately.

Can I choose a one-way or return flight?

Where the booking form offers the choice, you can select a return flight or a one-way flight. A one-way selection covers the outbound journey only, so you must arrange your return journey separately. Confirm flight inclusions with us before paying.

Are flights, accommodation, visas, and activities included?

Inclusions can vary by tour package. Do not assume that flights, accommodation, visa fees, meals, transfers, or activities are included unless the specific package confirms them. Ask us to clarify the inclusions and any exclusions before booking.

What if I need to change or cancel my booking?

Contact us as soon as possible. Change, cancellation, and refund terms can depend on the package and suppliers involved, so ask for the applicable terms before confirming your booking or making payment.

How can I contact Jovick Travel & Tours?

Visit our Contact page to send an enquiry, email us, call, or use the WhatsApp option listed there.
